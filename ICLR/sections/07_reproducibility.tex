\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

Every number in this paper is derived from released artifacts. We release all 64{,}800
verification records with their raw model responses, the 1{,}800 generated candidates, the
full override audit, and the generation, verification, fuzzing, and reporting harnesses that
produced them, together with the analysis scripts for every table and figure. An anonymized
repository accompanies this submission (\url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/JUDGe-who-verifies-agents-6A34/}).

The details needed to re-run the grid are in the paper rather than only in the code.
Section~\ref{sec:setup-models} gives the four model identifiers, the serving provider, the
decoding temperature, the generation and verification token budgets, and the retry policy.
Section~\ref{sec:setup-tasks} gives the sampling seed and the per-item seed that fixes
science option order. Section~\ref{sec:setup-grid} specifies the factorial design.
Appendix~\ref{app:execution} specifies the execution harness, its resource limits, and the
differential fuzzer including the input proposer and the adjudicating oracle.
Appendix~\ref{app:prompts} gives the four prompt components verbatim, the ownership-frame
sentences, and the verdict parser with its fallback. Appendix~\ref{app:capability} gives the
item-adjusted estimator. Appendix~\ref{app:models} lists all model strings, parameter counts,
and serving providers.

Two caveats are worth stating plainly. Decoding at temperature~0 through a hosted inference
API is deterministic in intent but not bit-reproducible in practice: provider-side batching
and kernel nondeterminism can move individual verdicts between runs. We therefore release
the traces themselves rather than asking a reader to reproduce them call by call, and every
statistic in the paper is recomputable from those traces offline. Separately, the
GPQA-Diamond curators ask that items not be posted in plain text, to limit leakage into
future training corpora. Our science release therefore ships item identifiers, candidate
verdicts, and our own outputs, but not the benchmark questions; the exact 150-item sample
and its option ordering reconstruct from the gated source under the seeds given in
Section~\ref{sec:setup-tasks}. HumanEval+ and MATH items are redistributed in full under
their respective licenses.
