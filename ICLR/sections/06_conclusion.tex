\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Agreement is not verification. Across $64{,}800$ evaluations, ungrounded judges share correlated blind spots, miss errors from stronger generators, and exhibit an 11.9\,pp self-preference gap driven by belief persistence rather than prompt framing or recognition. Model-side fixes—blinding, scaling, voting, and debate—cannot overcome flawed generative priors. Environmental execution grounding uniquely restores reliability, decorrelating verifier errors and achieving a 93.3\% catch rate. Ultimately, reliable verification depends on ground-truth environment signals, not more persuasive readers.
